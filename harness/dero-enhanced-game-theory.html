1What the suite is

A DERO smart contract cannot see who mined a block and cannot change the block reward. What it can do is hold DERO, enforce rules that nobody can change after deployment, and — the key idea of Round 33 — verify a block it is given: it stores the id of the block a call runs in, later accepts that block's raw bytes only if their SHA3-256 hash equals the stored id, and then reads each miniblock inside. A miner that writes an 8-byte game tag into its miniblocks can therefore be credited by proof, not by vote. The suite builds games on top of that.

It can

  • credit a member only for miniblocks it really mined, checked against the block's own hash (gt_pool);
  • pay rewards linear in verified work, with a bounded stake boost and a per-identity cap;
  • burn part of every inflow into a balance no entrypoint can send (provable with DERO.GetSC);
  • publish a public miner-concentration signal from the proven blocks;
  • pay independent auditors who re-derive a chain fact, without letting a majority override the sponsor (gt_audit);
  • run no-loss prize rounds and a notary with a bounded fee rebate; advertise the games pull-only (gt_beacon).

It cannot (the honest limits)

  • raise or redirect the block reward: it is CalcBlockReward(height) in consensus code;
  • see the P2P network: eclipse attacks, selfish mining and reorgs are invisible to it (an orphaned block's anchor is reverted);
  • prevent collusion among auditors who all repeat a wrong answer, or bribery (it only makes them unprofitable to grief);
  • show a hashrate effect yet: every measured member so far is the operator's own wallet; that needs outside miners;
  • stop one operator from splitting into several identities (each pays the entry fee; the cap protects only against one dominant identity);
  • mint DERO: every send is checked against the contract's own balance.

Does a smart contract need two wallets?

No. Deploying is one transaction from one wallet: the install runs Initialize under that transaction and the owner is its signer at ring size 2; the other ring member is a random registered account of the chain, not a second wallet of yours. A game between two parties needs two wallets, one per player; a disputed attestation needs a third (a juror may be neither the claimer nor the challenger). blockchain/transaction_execute.go · walletapi/daemon_communication.go (child rft §11a)

2How the verified pool works

Follow the numbered steps (press ▶ to play them). Every box quotes the line of gt_pool.bas that does it: hover or focus its tag.

The reward formula

Wi = Σcredited miniblocks (100 + boost), boost = min(100 · stake / S, 100)

payouti(e) = min(P0e · Wi / TWe, 30 % · P0e)

Weights are linear in verified work, so splitting work or stake over several addresses never gains (a split pays one more entry fee of 1,000 atomic instead). Stake counts only after it has been locked for one epoch (400 blocks) and at most doubles a work share; stake without mining earns nothing. Claims open 100 blocks after the epoch ends and close when Rollover opens, four epochs later. SRC

Money in, money out

  • In: Register pays exactly the entry fee (1,000 atomic); Fund(e) adds sponsor DERO to epoch e.
  • Burn: 20 % of every inflow goes to burned, which no entrypoint sends — the balance is always ≥ burned.
  • Prover fee: a proof that credits at least one miniblock pays its sender 2,000 atomic from the epoch pool (someone must pay for the ~1.7 M-gas call).
  • Stake: Lock / Unlock after 2,000 blocks; stake is never burned and never pays the pool.
  • Out: Claim per member and epoch; the rest of a closed epoch rolls into the current one. There is no owner withdrawal and no code update.

The audit bounty: check, don't vote

Facts that are not inside a block (an emission total, a set of balances) are paid for by gt_audit: the sponsor commits to its own answer, auditors commit to theirs bound to their address, and after the reveal window anyone settles. A majority can never override the sponsor; a disagreement only marks the round disputed and refunds everyone — so Sybils can grief a round but never profit from it.

4What miners joining through the suite gives the network

An honest account, not a promise: what the contracts measurably do for DERO, and where the evidence stops. Each figure below is read from a record or the child rft when the page is built.

(a) Security and (b) forensics

The pool pays only work that ended in main-chain blocks: a side block's transactions never execute, so it is never anchored or credited — orphan-causing behaviour is unpaid. More independent miners raise the hashrate an attacker needs for a 51 % or selfish-mining attack. Every proven block leaves public facts in the contract: the header timestamp (42 verified timestamps in epoch 19,790) and each miniblock's KeyHash, counted per epoch against the 40 % concentration alarm HARNESS. A contract cannot see the P2P network: eclipse attacks and reorgs stay invisible to it HARNESS.

(c) Deflation and (d) the hashrate effect

41,000 atomic DERO are burned into a balance no entrypoint can send HARNESS — a cost the burners pay, not a payment to miners. Under free entry, hashrate follows real value: an incentive I per block raises hashrate by about I / R (R = 30,750 atomic per block, so +1 % needs ≈ 308 atomic of real value per block) HARNESS. The games routed 0.27–0.59 % of the reward to miners as fees HARNESS, which bounds the effect at that size — and only if outside miners respond. No outside miner has joined yet; every credit so far is the operator's wallet HARNESS.

   (task, from, upto), e.g. task 1 = block-header digest, task 2 = emission digest. The contract never interprets
   the task. The sponsor's own answer is HIDDEN: Post stores commit = HEX(SHA256(answer + ":" + salt)).

1 · Simulator tests

The in-process simulator credits deposits without debiting the sender: count what the contract sends and holds. One regression test per audit finding.

2 · Invariants

For every contract: conservation (balance = a liability formula of its own storage), no excess payout, one-time actions, access control, liveness (every participant can get out) and bounds (gas and Uint64 at the documented maxima), checked after every call of randomized sequences against an independent Go model (gametheory/audit/r33_verify.md).

The same playbook on a private simulator chain gates the code before real DERO touches it. Its numbers are never results: at difficulty 1 shares follow the number of miner processes, not hashrate (child rft lesson 0).

Re-derive every payout from the chain (block bytes, the contract's own keys via DERO.GetSC) with a tool that does not share code with the driver, and audit each epoch again independently.

11The mechanisms

M1 · Miner-attested token (MAT): optimistic bonded claim, challenge, commit-reveal jurors GO

Contract gt_attest.bas · entrypoints Initialize, SetParams, Freeze, Claim, Challenge, Stake, Unstake, CommitVote, RevealVote, Finalize, ClaimJuror, WithdrawTreasury

Equilibrium. Truthful claiming is the attractor iff bond > (1-c) * reward * v / c per miniblock, c = P(challenge) * P(jury correct) (econsim m1_critical_bond, replicator). Jurors: coherent voting earns a share of the loser's bond plus slashed stakes; incoherent or unrevealed jurors lose ceil(slash %) of their stake (Schelling point). R33: a juror's whole free stake is escrowed per dispute at CommitVote (GT-A02), so one stake backs one open dispute; the coherent jurors' pot is the losing side's unpaid bond plus floor(slash %) of the incoherent escrow, never more than the loser escrowed.

Sustainability. MAT is issued, not bought: its value is what fee payers and sponsors in M2/M3 pay for the boost. Bonds and slashes are zero-sum inside one dispute. Needs at least one honest verifier-challenger and juror participation. R33 GT-A07: up to 8 challengers per claim until claim height + window; an undecided dispute (yes == no, including no reveal) refunds every bond and mints nothing. Slashes no coherent juror can claim and floor remainders go to an owner-withdrawable treasury (GT-A13).

Attack surface (8)
  • sybil: a miner may split into many addresses; claims are per address and per height range, so splitting gains nothing
  • collusion / P+epsilon bribe of jurors (econsim m1_bribe_cost); mitigation: commit-reveal, slashing, stake caps; residual risk stated
  • griefing: a challenger loses its bond to an upheld honest claim
  • front-running / copying: votes are committed as sha256(i:vote:salt:ADDRESS_STRING(SIGNER())) (R33 GT-A08), so a copied commit cannot be revealed; claims and challenges are public by design
  • gas DoS: every entrypoint O(1) except Finalize, which loops over at most maxch = 8 challengers
  • R33 GT-A09 (design, residual): an unchallenged claim mints after `window` blocks; MAT integrity needs a watcher online within every window. M3 · No-loss prize pool with a commit-reveal draw and a MAT boost GO

Contract gt_prize.bas · entrypoints Initialize, SetParams, SetMAT, Freeze, StakeMAT, UnstakeMAT, Fund, Enter, Reveal, Draw, Withdraw, SetFees, WithdrawTreasury

Equilibrium. Entry EV = prize * w / W - entry fee - tx fees >= 0 (principal and, if revealed, the reveal bond are returned); the MAT boost doubles a miner's weight. R33 GT-A05: a forfeited reveal bond goes to the treasury, never into the prize, so withholding k reveals costs k * rbond for at most the prize: unprofitable while prize <= rbond (a Sybil with k entries chooses among 2^k outcomes, so the bound is per withheld reveal, not rbond * (n-1)); deployments keep rbond >= the expected prize. Sustainability. Prizes are sponsor funds only (R33 GT-A05). Forfeited reveal bonds and entry fees go to an owner-withdrawable treasury; never another participant's principal.

Attack surface (5)

  • v1 only: integrator block withholding to re-roll RANDOM (removed in v2)

  • R33 GT-A01/A12/A13/A15: Initialize runs once and never anonymously; no-deposit entrypoints refuse DERO; rbond, fee, minp <= 10^12, boost <= 1000; a copied prize commit only gives the copier the same reveal option every entrant has (commit not address-bound, unchanged)

M4 · Audit bounty: paid independent detection over public chain data (headers, emission) GO

Contract gt_audit.bas · entrypoints Initialize, SetParams, Freeze, Post, Enter, RevealSponsor, Reveal, Settle, Withdraw, Reclaim

Equilibrium. Honest auditing pays iff bounty / winners > the auditor's fees (econsim m4_auditor_ev); a lazy guess or a copied commit forfeits the bond (the commit is bound to ADDRESS_STRING(SIGNER())). R33 GT-A04: auditors whose answer equals the sponsor's revealed answer share the bounty only if no revealed answer differs; any differing answer sets disputed=1 (public detection signal), refunds every bond and returns the bounty to the sponsor (Reclaim). A majority never overrides the sponsor and never slashes a minority: Sybils can grief (dispute) but never profit. NOT prevented: collusion or bribery, a single entrant's grief-dispute.

Sustainability. Bounties are the sponsor's own funds per round plus bonds forfeited by non-revealers and remainders (a rollover, 'carry', funded by earlier rounds); never another participant's bond or a later deposit (econsim ledger_m4, stop-inflow PASS). A disputed or sponsor-withheld round refunds every bond and returns the bounty to the sponsor (R33 GT-A04). An honest auditor gains iff bounty / winners exceeds its own fees (econsim M4 checks).

Attack surface (7)
  • Copying a commit: refused, the commit is bound to ADDRESS_STRING(SIGNER()) and Reveal recomputes it (DVM test CopyAttack).
  • Lazy guessing: a 64-hex digest cannot be guessed; a non-revealer forfeits its bond; a wrong revealed answer disputes the round (refund, no profit; R33 GT-A04).
  • Sponsor withholding: every bond is refunded (refund=1); the bounty returns to the sponsor only after the timeout (SponsorNoReveal).
  • Sybil majority against a revealed sponsor answer (R33 GT-A04): disputed=1, every bond refunded, bounty back to the sponsor; the Sybils gain nothing (TestR33AuditA04SybilMajority).
  • Gas / storage-gas lock (R33 GT-A03): answers are 1..256 bytes and tallied per HEX(SHA3256(answer)) at Reveal; Settle is O(n), measured 3.1M compute gas at maxn = 32 (TestR33AuditA03SettleLineLimit). A round never settled times out at start + commitw + revealw + tmo (2000): entrants pull their bonds back with Withdraw, the sponsor the bounty with Reclaim.
  • NOT prevented: collusion or bribery among auditors who all repeat a wrong sponsor answer; any single entrant can force a dispute (grief, at the cost of its locked bond and fees).
  • R33 GT-A01/A12/A13/A15: Initialize runs once and never anonymously; no-deposit entrypoints refuse DERO; bond <= 10^12, windows <= 10^6, maxn <= 32.
