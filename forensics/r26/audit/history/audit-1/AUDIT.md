# Independent audit of forensics/r26

Auditor: a second implementation (`audit2-1`, `harness/forensics/audit2.py` on the primitives of `audit_check.py`) that shares no code with the v2 builder. Refute by default (rft §0.8.5).

Bound to the dataset data `2eca35695f5e0877a8f267132fb11c49fb417c37310f85afa166f04754de3879` (26 files outside audit/), audited from manifest `181ab956a6644433d69f4da35e5edc7cfc1e6bea0d3dc928e4cf8072f727dfb6`. Rerun block SHA-256 `7253beb74d67cd9a492b30a4ad63ab9e02ecac09b564ff0ebae7e9b15704c078`.

**Result: 24 challenges, 19 HOLD, 4 FAIL (overturned), 1 UNRESOLVED; overturns over all runs of this version: 4 (distinct claims overturned: 4).**

## What was re-run

Every v1 store block and every v2 wall-1 block was decoded by this implementation and hashed (SHA3-256 under our own Keccak); the tips walk runs from our anchor through the wall-1 link. Every tx body was re-hashed and its TXID re-computed from the core statement. Fees, burns and SC counts were summed per block. Body copies from every independent holder were compared byte for byte. Every proof-carrying body went through the structural verifier. The node replay from the archive seed was re-run through the sync path's HF3 entry on a fresh APFS clone with our own plan. Only after that were the builder's derived/ files read, as claims.

## Overturned and unresolved

- **D2-01 FAILS**: the ledger entry of r26 names the manifest the dataset now has. The open version's ledger entry was not re-registered after the manifest was re-written (the class files were added at the merge); verify_dataset.py checked L2 for sealed versions only, so it passed. Fixed at source: rule L9 (an open version's manifest equals its ledger entry), KAT AU1 + mutation; the ledger re-registered. Affects: V2-FEES, V2-DUP-BODY, V2-END-STATE, V-STATE-ROOTS, V-DC2.
- **D2-14 FAILS**: wall 4: the node's own checks reject block 7,467,546 (tx not in the HF3 table), so the replay ends at 7,467,545 and the end-state check is pending. Overturned: the stop is an artifact of the replay tool, which called Add_Complete_Block without the HF3 table step (the merge found it through TestR23bAddCompleteBlockCensus, B123). Through the sync path's entry the node accepts every block to our anchor and ends at our own treehash. The dataset still carries the pre-fix runs and "pending" end-state claims: they are corrected only by a rebuilt v2 (B123), never in place. Affects: V2-END-STATE, V-STATE-ROOTS.
- **D2-16 UNRESOLVED**: A10c (carried from v1): the archive roots 7,453,290..7,467,515 are the canonical roots of those topos. The audit's own replay binds the archive top root to our treehash (the replay from it reaches ca11cc21), which is the end-state check A10c asked for; it stays UNRESOLVED for this dataset because v2 does not carry that run (B123: rebuild v2 with it, then it can HOLD). Affects: V-STATE-ROOTS.
- **D2-19 FAILS**: classes.json DC3 figures reflect dataset v2 (fees and public burns of the window). The figures are honest lower bounds over v1 (G31 holds), but stale: v2 settled every pending block, so the window fee and burn figures can be exact; classes.json still reads v1 (base r25, B126). Affects: V2-FEES.
- **D2-20 FAILS**: G32 records half: every v2 derived/raw record carries its defect class. B126: classes.py tags the v1 record layout, so G32's record check passes vacuously on v2. Affects: V2-FEES, V2-DUP-BODY, V2-END-STATE, V-STATE-ROOTS, V-DC2.

## NO-GO (rft §9)

- **NOGO-B97-SOUNDNESS**: a second, independent SOUNDNESS verifier of the range proof / inner-product argument. telling two verifiers apart, or showing the defect class at work, needs a proof that one verifier accepts and an honest one rejects: exploit content (rft §0.8.1 rule 6, §5, §9). Re-running honest verification a second time adds nothing that D2-12 and the node verifier (v1 A11) do not already show, and needs the ring state, which exists only inside the held archive (v1 A11: 2 txs). Recorded as NO-GO; not attempted. Instead: the structural / public-value verifier D2-12 (pass/fail per named check only).

## Limits

- The structural verifier shows encodings and layout, not soundness. Proof soundness (DC2) stays NOT-DECIDABLE.
- The duplicate-body census sees only the copies held; a second body under a single-source TXID is unobservable.
- The audit's own replay is audit evidence, not a dataset file; the dataset is corrected only by a new build (B123).
- Hidden amounts are ElGamal ciphertexts. No grade here says anything about them.
